The studio

Two wheels, one kiln, an arch under the railway.

Hollowell started in 2019 when two potters pooled their savings on a second-hand kiln and a lease on Arch 14. Everything in the shop was thrown, trimmed, glazed and fired here by the same four hands. Trains go over about every eight minutes. The pots do not mind.
