Mobile section editing

A phone-first take on the section editing modes mockup. Open this page on a phone to try it full screen.

  • Tap a section to select it. Its tools appear at the bottom of the screen.
  • Layout opens a sheet of mobile layouts and colours, with the section still in view above it.
  • Content lets you type straight into text and swap photos, including from your phone.
  • Arrange replaces freeform dragging: tap an item, then move it up or down.
  • Add adds to the section or adds a new section below it. More moves, duplicates or deletes it.
  • Tools switches between three places for the editing tools: docked at the bottom of the screen, along the top of the selected section, or down its right-hand side. On the section, they stay in view as you scroll.
  • Phone | Desktop in the top bar switches to the same page laid out for a desktop screen. On a computer you can keep editing there, with the same tools in a bar at the bottom of the selected section. On a phone it's a preview.
