Pages and menus

Most people with a simple site think "I have pages, and some of them are in my menu", or that the menu is their pages. These prototypes test ways for the Pages screen to show that directly, with the site beside it, including the automatic Page List menus WordPress makes today. Each one starts the same way every time; Reset puts it back.

  • A · One list of pagesThe recommended directionEvery page in one list. A marker on each says whether it's in the header menu; click it for what that means and the one action that changes it.
  • B · Automatic menuWhat most block-theme sites have todayThe same list on a site whose menu shows every page automatically. Taking a page out switches the menu to one you choose, after a single confirm.
  • C · Header and footer menusMore than one menuThe same list on a site with two menus. The marker's popover becomes a checklist of menus, so the list never splits.
  • D · Menu and other pagesComparison: two sectionsThe earlier take: "In your menu" and "Other pages" as two groups, with add and remove buttons.
  • E · Menu in page settingsControl: decide on the pageClose to WordPress today: a table of pages with a Menu column, an "Add to menu" switch when creating a page, and menu settings inside each page.
  • F · All pages with menu switchesA switch on every pageEvery page in one list, with an "In menu" switch on each. The menu follows the list's order: drag a page to reorder both.

Test scripts, tasks and what to watch for are in the README. The bakery and its pages are placeholders.
