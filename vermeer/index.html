From looking at your site to changing it

The editor opens with your site on view, as visitors see it. To change it, you press Edit. Someone who has just made their first site may not know to look there, and may click on the thing they want to change instead, with nothing happening. These five prototypes are the same small bookshop site in the same editor, drawn after Create not Learn's, all with the Edit button. Four add one more way in, through the site itself, so a test can tell whether a site that responds to the first click or hover gets people into editing faster, and with fewer wrong turns, than a button they have to find.

  • 0 · ControlOnly the Edit button, as Create not Learn has todayClicking the site does what a visitor's click does, and nothing else. The baseline for the others.
  • A1 · Click to editA click on anything that isn't a link or button goes straight inThe words clicked get the caret where the click was; a picture or background outlines its section. Links, buttons and boxes work as usual.
  • A2 · Ask on clickThe same click asks "Do you want to edit?"A small box at the click, with Edit and No. Edit lands as A1 does.
  • B · Hover noticeThe pointer on the site shows a notice wrapped round the Edit button, straight away"Trying to change something on this page?", in a card around the toolbar's Edit, whose copy of Edit sits exactly where the real one is, to show where it is, and a × that keeps it away until the next time out of editing.
  • C · Choose on clickEvery click offers its own action or Edit"Go to Events" or "Edit this", "Use this button" or "Edit this", "Keep looking" or "Edit this page", in a box at the click.

Add ?notes (or #notes) to any of them for the facilitator panel, or open the control, A1, A2, B or C with it: it lists the tasks, counts how and when people went into editing, and logs every click, prompt and change. Finished, in the bar at the bottom, gives a code to paste into a testing tool. Everything is kept in the browser, so a reload carries on where it was; use Reset in the panel to start again. The test script is in the README. The bookshop and its pages are placeholders.
